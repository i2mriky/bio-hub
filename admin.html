Ask NM*

The assistant on the site. It may only use the facts below plus your results, tools and numbers. One fact per line. The key lives in the Cloudflare Worker, never here.

The assistant can't see images or videos. When you add a section, a room, a clip or a case, add one line here saying what it shows, or the assistant won't know it exists.

Work cards the assistant can show as pictures are in data.json → assistant.works (id, title, line, img, go = section id). Ask Claude to add new ones when new work goes up.
